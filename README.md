# 📈 Business Performance Analysis & Reporting

I'm Sneha Patel, and this is my third project from my 
Python with AI Internship Bootcamp — and honestly my 
favourite one so far!

By this point I was more comfortable with Pandas and 
Seaborn, so I focused on making the visualizations 
actually look clean and professional. This one feels 
closest to what a real junior data analyst would do 
at a company.

## What does it do?
Analyzes 1194 business sales transactions to help a company 
understand where their money is coming from:
- Which states are most profitable?
- Which products bring the most revenue?
- Which payment mode generates the highest profit?
- How are orders trending month by month?

## What I learned
- Combining multiple groupby operations to get deeper insights
- Building horizontal bar charts, line plots, and bar charts
- Combining two dataframes side by side using pd.concat
- Exporting clean summary reports for business use

## Key Findings
- Florida and New York were the top profit-generating states
- Markers and Tables had the highest total revenue
- Debit Card brought in the most profit among all payment modes
- Furniture was the most popular category by quantity sold
